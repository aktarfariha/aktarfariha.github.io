Land Suitability Analysis Applying Spatial Techniques of GIS

A GIS-based suitability analysis for identifying a potential location for a new health center in Ward 73 of Dhaka by integrating proximity and exclusion criteria for roads, religious places, shopping areas, existing health centers and educational institutes.

Objective Identify land that simultaneously satisfies accessibility requirements and separation constraints to support health-facility location planning within Ward 73.
Study area Ward 73 · Dhaka
Software ArcMap 10.8
Core techniques Buffer Analysis · Spatial Overlay · Selection by Location · Suitability Modeling

Suitability criteria

Five spatial conditions used in the analysis

01 · Accessibility Roads

Candidate land should be within 22 m of nearby roads.

02 · Accessibility Religious places

Candidate land should be within 82 m of religious places.

03 · Accessibility Shopping areas

Candidate land should be within 54 m of shopping areas.

04 · Separation Existing health centers

Candidate land should be beyond 104 m from existing health centers.

05 · Separation Educational institutes

Candidate land should be beyond 104 m from educational institutes.

Workflow

From spatial features to a suitable site

01 Map relevant features

Roads, religious places, shopping areas, health centers and educational institutes were represented spatially.

02 Apply distance criteria

Proximity and exclusion buffers were generated according to the specified distance thresholds.

03 Overlay eligible areas

Spatial conditions were combined to retain locations satisfying all accessibility and separation requirements.

04 Identify suitable land

The final output highlights the candidate area that meets the complete set of suitability criteria.

Project outcome

The ArcMap workflow combined five location criteria into a single land-suitability model, producing a final map of the area considered suitable for a new health center in Ward 73 of Dhaka.
